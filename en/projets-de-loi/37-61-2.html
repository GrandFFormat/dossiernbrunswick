Bill 37: Supplementary Appropriations Act 2024-2025 (2)

Received Royal Assent on June 12, 2026 · Sponsor: René Legacy

  • Authorizes up to $121,503,446.55 in extra spending from the Consolidated Fund for fiscal year 2024-2025
  • General Government receives an additional $61,097,114.24
  • Department of Health receives an additional $57,618,528.13
  • Regional Development Corporation receives an additional $2,061,582.07
  • Department of Tourism, Heritage and Culture receives an additional $30,900.79
  • Department of Education and Early Childhood Development receives $695,321.32 for capital spending
  • Covers public service expenses not otherwise provided for between April 1, 2024 and March 31, 2025
Who voted for or against

No list of names exists for this law. It was passed by voice: members answer from their seats, and only the outcome is written in the Assembly’s Journal. No one is counted.

A list of names exists only for a recorded division: each member is then counted for or against, and their name is written in the Journal. The bill on the Assembly’s website.

✋ What is “challenging”?

One click on “Ask for an explanation” = a citizen request for the bill's sponsor to explain it in plain language, and for us to keep an eye on it together.

An account (email) is required — one request per person, no anonymous requests.

In New Brunswick, these requests lead to nothing official: the Assembly has no online petitions, unlike Quebec or the House of Commons. For now, it is a simple way to see which laws trouble people. Some day, an elected member may find a use for it.
