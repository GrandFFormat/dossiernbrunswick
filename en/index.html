Acting on a bill

There is no online petition in New Brunswick

The Assembly only accepts petitions on paper, signed by hand and presented by a member. No button, here or anywhere else, carries official weight. These three routes do.

Write to your member

The one route that always works. Every member has an address at the Assembly.

The official list →

Have a paper petition presented

Signed by hand, then handed to your member, who presents it in the House. A minister must reply in writing within two weeks — but that reply is not published.

Our mission

The official site is the source. This one makes it easier to follow.

legnb.ca does

  • Organizes everything by official document
  • The complete, legal source for all of it
  • Neutral — never compares, never summarizes

This site tries to do

  • Organizes everything by person: one page, all their files
  • Turns the jargon into plain language (see the Glossary)
  • Lets you follow what affects you, with no scores and no verdicts
