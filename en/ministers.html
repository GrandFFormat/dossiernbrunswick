Reading the vote attendance

Full definition → Glossary

100 %: of the 3 recorded divisions held since the session opened, this person voted in 100 % of them — yea or nay, whichever way.

0 %100 %
  • Green (39) — 100 % and above, the Assembly's median.
  • Yellow (0) — from 94 % to 99 %, below the median.
  • No colour (0) — under 94 %.
  • n/a (9) — no recorded division on file for this person. That is not an absence: we simply do not know.
  • does not vote — the Speaker of the House (Francine Landry) chairs the sittings and stays neutral. That name appears in no recorded division, and that is the rule.

Among the 39 people counted: from 100 % to 100 %. The figure rests on 3 recorded divisions, held over 1 sitting day.

  • Recorded divisions are rare in New Brunswick. Most bills pass on a voice vote, with nobody named. While a session holds only a few, this rate measures almost nothing — it sharpens as divisions pile up.
  • The Speaker does not vote. The Speaker chairs the House and stays neutral: that name appears in no recorded division, and that is as it should be.
  • This is not an official measure. The Assembly publishes no attendance rate. This one is worked out here, from the names printed in the Journals, and a missed vote never says why.
