Constituency office budget
$72,666

These are not salaries. The sessional allowance is part of the pay; the rest reimburses costs — mileage, air fare, committees, travel. The constituency office budget pays for an office and its staff, not the person. Members of the Executive Council do not appear in the Assembly’s expense table. Only their constituency office budget is published there. A fiscal year starts on 1 April: this one may hold amounts paid before the current legislature.
