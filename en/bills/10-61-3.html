Bill 10

An Act to Amend the Education Act

Government Bill

Sponsored by Claire Johnson · Liberal Party · Moncton South

In plain words

  • Adds a definition for "French immersion pathway" to the Education Act
  • Defines it as a program in English-language schools taught mainly in French for second language learning
  • Allows superintendents to implement a French immersion pathway in a school, following Minister's policies
  • Pupils may normally start the French immersion pathway in grade 1 or grade 6
  • Superintendent may allow a pupil to start at a different grade if a parent requests it
