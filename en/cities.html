No city is covered

The cities

This site reads the Legislative Assembly, not city councils. Nothing decided in a New Brunswick city hall is published here today.

What a municipal section would hold

  • The agenda of every council sitting, in plain language.
  • Who voted how, where the minutes say so.
  • The by-laws under study, and the stage they have reached.

On one condition

The same one as for the Assembly: that the city publish its documents in a way that can be read honestly and refreshed automatically. A city behind an anti-robot wall will not be covered — we do not work around a protection, even when the documents are public.

Sponsorship

Sponsor your city

Following a city council — agendas, votes and resolutions in plain language — takes reading work for each city. A local sponsorship would pay for one city for a year.

No city is covered, and no price is set for New Brunswick. There is nothing to sell today: this is an intention, not an offer.
