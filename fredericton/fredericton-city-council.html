Fredericton City Council

The members

Fredericton City Council has thirteen members: the Mayor, elected by the whole city, and twelve Councillors, one per ward. They were elected on May 11, 2026 and sworn in on June 1. Contact details come from the City’s website; absences, from the list of members present in each set of minutes. The minutes name those who voted against only when the vote is divided: seven times in 2026. Their decisions are on the Decisions page.
