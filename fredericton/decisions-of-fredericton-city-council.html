Decisions of Fredericton City Council

Here are the decisions of Fredericton City Council: each card is a resolution from the minutes — a staff recommendation, a by-law, an appointment or a member’s motion — grouped by meeting. Open a card to read its text, who moved it, who seconded it and the result. The minutes name those who voted against only when the vote is divided. The members are on the City Council page.

What a card contains. The title and text of the resolution, as the City’s minutes give them, who moved it, who seconded it, and the result. The minutes name those who voted against only when the vote is divided. Each card links to the official minutes of the meeting, in PDF.
