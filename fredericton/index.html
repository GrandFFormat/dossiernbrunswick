What this site covers

After each meeting, the City of Fredericton publishes the minutes in English and in French. That document is what you see here, resolution by resolution: its title, its text, who moved it, who seconded it, and the result. The meetings are those of the full Council, regular and special.

Fredericton’s minutes give no vote count: a resolution is “carried” or “defeated”. When the vote is divided, they name those who voted against, and so does the card. Nothing is made up in place of the count. Absences come from the list of members present at each meeting.

The vocabulary (rezoning, consent agenda, by-law, third reading) is explained in the glossary, with the number of times each term appears in the period. The Sources page says where every number comes from and what a machine can miss.
