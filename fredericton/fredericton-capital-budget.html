How to read this page

  • The capital budget is not the whole City budget. It covers investments — what gets built or bought — and not day-to-day operations (salaries, police, fire, garbage collection), which are in the operating budget.
  • The areas (Mobility, Livable Community…) are the City’s own “results areas”. In each one, the City separates renewing what exists (Renewal/Rebuild), additions (New/Extensions) and what serves the city’s growth (Growth).
  • Why 2025. The 2026 budget book is not online: the City provides it on request from the City Clerk’s office. The 2027 one, adopted on November 9, 2026, has not been published either. This page will be updated when a newer book is.
  • What is not here: the water and sewer capital budget, which is a separate fund, and the property tax rates. This page does not estimate your tax bill.
