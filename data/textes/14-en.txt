BILL 14An Act Respecting Property Tax Reform MeasuresHis Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Legislative Assembly of New Brunswick, enacts as follows:Assessment Act 1( 1) Section 8 of the Assessment Act, chapter A-14 of the Revised Statutes, 1973, is repealed and the following is substituted: 8( 1) To enable the Director to make a proper assessment of real property or to reconsider an assessment in respect of real property, the Director shall at all reasonable times and on reasonable requests be given free access to all real property and to all parts of every building situated on the real property and to all reports, records, financial statements, statistics or other relevant information in the possession or control of the owner, occupier or user of the real property that the Director considers necessary.8( 2) Despite subsection (1), the Director may at all reasonable times enter on private lands for the purpose of carrying out an inspection of the lands and making an assessment or reconsidering an assessment of the real property without entry into a building.8( 3) In assessing real property, the Director may request and may rely on reports, records, financial statements, statistics and other relevant information in the possession or control of the owner, occupier or user of the real property that the Director considers necessary and that relate to the ownership, quantity, nature, location, extent and value of the real property. 8( 4) An assessment of real property referred to in subsection (3) at its real and true value may be made by means of examining the reports, records, financial statements, statistics or other information referred to in that subsection, and the assessment shall be deemed to be a valid and proper assessment for all purposes of this Act and the Real Property Tax Act despite that no other means were used to assess the real property at its real and true value and that an inspection of the real property has not been carried out.8( 5) Every owner, user or occupier of real property shall provide the Director, by mail or by electronic means and within 45 days of the sending of the request, all the information requested by the Director that will assist the Director in making a proper assessment of the real property or reconsidering an assessment of real property.8( 6) A request under subsections (1), (3) and (5) shall be deemed to have been received by the owner, user or occupier of the real property on the day the request was sent by the Director.8( 7) Proof of sending a request under subsection (1), (3) or (5) may be made by a certificate purporting to be signed by the Director naming the person to whom the request was sent and specifying the time, place and manner of the sending of the request.8( 8) A document that purports to be a certificate of the Director under subsection (7) is admissible in evidence before any court, judge or board and when adduced is, in the absence of evidence to the contrary, proof of the facts stated in the certificate without proof of the appointment, signature or authority of the Director.8( 9) When a person in whose name the assessment is made fails to comply with this section, the Director shall determine the real and true value of the real property in question.8( 10) Despite any other provision of this Act, a person who, having been required to provide information under this section, fails to provide the information is not entitled to request that the Director review the real property assessment under section 25 for the year in which the information under this section was requested.8( 11) No person shall have any right of action for trespass resulting from entry on private lands authorized under this section.1( 2) The
