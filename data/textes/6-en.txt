BILL 6An Act to Amend the Employment Standards ActHis Majesty, by and with the advice and consent of the Legislative Assembly of New Brunswick, enacts as follows:1 Section 38.9 of the Employment Standards Act, chapter E-7.2 of the Acts of New Brunswick, 1982, is amended(a) in subsection (1) ( i) in the portion preceding the definition “foreign worker” by striking out “in this section and in section 38.91” and substituting “in this section and in sections 38.901, 38.902 and 38.91”; ( ii) by adding the following definitions in alphabetical order: “family member” means, in relation to any person, (membre de la famille)(a) the spouse of that person,(b) the parent of that person or of that person’s spouse, (c) the child of that person or of that person’s spouse, (d) the sibling of that person or of that person’s spouse, (e) the sibling of a parent of that person or of that person’s spouse, (f) the child of a sibling of that person or of that person’s spouse, (g) the first cousin of that person or of that person’s spouse, or (h) the grandparent of that person or of that person’s spouse.“foreign worker recruiter” means a person who provides foreign worker recruitment services. (recruteur de travailleurs étrangers)“foreign worker recruitment services” means services to assist a foreign worker to secure employment in the Province or to assist an employer to secure employment in the Province for a foreign worker, including (services de recrutement de travailleurs étrangers)(a) finding or attempting to find employment in the Province for a foreign worker, (b) assisting or advising an employer in the hiring of a foreign worker, and(c) assisting or advising another person in taking the actions listed in paragraph (a) or (b).“spouse” means, in relation to any person, (conjoint)(a) a person to whom that person is married, or (b) a person with whom that person cohabits and has cohabited continuously for at least one year in a conjugal relationship.(b) by repealing subsection (2) and substituting the following: 38.9( 2) Subject to subsection (2.1), an employer who recruits a foreign worker or engages the services of a foreign worker recruiter shall register with the Director in accordance with this section. (c) by adding after subsection (2) the following: 38.9( 2.1) The Minister may establish eligibility requirements to be met by the employer in order to apply to be registered. (d) in subsection (4) by repealing the portion preceding paragraph (a) and substituting the following: 38.9( 4) An employer that meets the eligibility requirements established under subsection (2.1), if any, shall apply to be registered by providing to the Director, in the form and manner required by the Director, the following information: (e) by adding after subsection (4) the following: 38.9( 4.1) On receiving an application under subsection (4), the Director may approve or refuse the application or place conditions on the registration of an employer. (f) by repealing subsection (5) and substituting the following: 38.9( 5) A registration is valid for one calendar year from the date the Director approves the application, and the employer shall provide an update of the information provided under subsection (4) each year on or before the anniversary date of the registration. (g) by adding after subsection (5) the following: 38.9( 5.1) The Director may suspend or revoke the registration of an employer at any time in accordance with the regulations. 38.9( 5.2) An employer whose application to be registered was refused or whose registration has been suspended or revoked may appeal the decision of the Director in accordance with the regulations. 38.9( 5.3) An employer or former employer of a foreign worker shall retain all books, records and documents relating to any foreign worker recruitment services provided by a foreign worker recruiter or the hiring of a foreign worker for a minimum period of three years after the date to which the books, records or documents relate. 38.9( 5.4) An employer shall deliver any of the books, records and documents that are required to be kept under subsection (5.3) to the Director at any time that the Director requires. (h) by repealing subsection (9). 2 The Act is amended by adding after section 38.9 the following: Foreign worker recruiter licence38.901( 1) On and after April 1, 2028, no person shall act as a foreign worker recruiter or engage in foreign worker recruitment services unless that person holds a foreign worker recruiter licence. 38.901( 2) Subsection (1) does not apply to (a) the Crown in right of the Province, any Crown corporation or any Crown agency, (b) a person acting in their capacity as an employee of the federal government, a municipal government, a public post-secondary educational institution or any portion of the public service of the Province as specified in Part 1, 2, 3 or 4 of the First Schedule of the Public Service Labour Relations Act, (c) a family member of a foreign worker who does not charge a fee for the provision of foreign worker recruitment services, or(d) any person or class of person prescribed by regulation. 38.901( 3) No person shall apply for a foreign worker recruiter licence unless (a) that person is an individual, and (b) that person meets the eligibility requirements prescribed by regulation and any requirements established by the Minister.38.901( 4) An application for a foreign worker recruiter licence shall(a) be provided to the Director in the form and manner specified by the Director, (b) contain the following information: ( i) the names and addresses of all the applicant’s partners, affiliates or agents; and ( ii) any other information prescribed by regulation, and(c) be accompanied by ( i) any fees prescribed by regulation, and ( ii) financial security if required by the Director. 38.901( 5) On receiving an application in accordance with subsection (4), the Director may issue a foreign worker recruiter licence to the applicant, subject to the terms and conditions prescribed by the regulations and any additional terms and conditions that the Director considers appropriate.38.901( 6) The Director may suspend or revoke a foreign worker recruiter licence at any time in accordance with the regulations.38.901( 7) A person to whom a foreign worker recruiter licence was not issued under subsection (5) or a licence holder whose licence has been suspended or revoked under subsection (6) may appeal the decision of the Director in accordance with the regulations.38.901( 8) A licence issued under this section is not transferable or assignable. 38.901( 9) A licence shall be issued for a term of no more than three years.38.901( 10) The Director may require a licence holder to file financial security with the Minister at any time during the term of a licence. 38.901( 11) Financial security filed under subparagraph (4)(c)(ii) or subsection (10) shall be in the amount and in the form prescribed by regulation. 38.901( 12) A licence holder shall retain all books, records and documents relating to any recruitment services provided for a minimum period of three years after the dates to which the books, records or documents relate. 38.901( 13) A licence holder shall deliver any of the books, records and documents that are required to be kept under subsection (12) to the Director at any time that the Director requires.Public register 38.902( 1) The Minister shall maintain a register of registered employers of foreign workers and foreign worker recruiter licence holders that contains the following information for each registration or licence: (a) the name and contact information of the registered employer or licence holder; (b) whether terms and conditions apply to the registration or licence; (c) whether the registration or licence is suspended or revoked and the date the suspension or revocation took effect; and (d) any other information prescribed by regulation. 38.902( 2) The register shall be made available to the public in the form and manner that the Minister considers appropriate. 3 Section 38.91 of the Act is amended (a) by repealing subsection (1) and substituting
