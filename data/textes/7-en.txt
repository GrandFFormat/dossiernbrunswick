salvage dealers to purchase or receive(a) copper wire that is not in its original manufactured state, or(b) copper wire whose insulation has been removed, burned or otherwise damaged.11.2( 2) No salvage dealer shall purchase or receive as salvage stranded copper wire consisting of eight or more strands except from the holder of a Class A licence who provides the salvage dealer with a photocopy of the licence. 11.2( 3) No salvage dealer shall purchase or receive as salvage stranded copper wire consisting of less than eight strands except from the following persons:(a) a holder of a Class A licence who provides to the salvage dealer a photocopy of the licence;(b) a holder of a Class B licence who provides to the salvage dealer a photocopy of the licence;(c) an individual who provides to the salvage dealer a signed and dated statement, in a form provided by the Minister, stating where the individual obtained the copper wire and setting out the individual’s name and address.11.2( 4) Any salvage dealer who has purchased or received copper as salvage shall withhold payment for 48 hours following the transaction.11.2( 5) Any salvage dealer who has purchased or received copper as salvage shall not resell, process, transform or destroy it for 48 hours following the transaction.11.2( 6) For the purposes of subsections (2) and (3), an agent of a holder of a electric utility as defined in the Electricity Act (entreprise de services publics) .“telecommunications service provider” means telecommunications service provider as defined in the Telecommunications Act (Canada). (fournisseur de services de télécommunication)11.4( 2) A public utility, a telecommunications service provider or any other business may apply to the Minister for a Class A licence authorizing the public utility, telecommunications service provider or other business to sell or deliver, as salvage, stranded copper wire consisting of eight or more strands to a salvage dealer.11.4( 3) An application form for a Class A licence shall include the following information:(a) the legal name of the business, including the corporate name or firm name, as well as any business name under which the business is carried on;(b) the contact information of persons responsible for the management of the business; and(c) information proving ownership of the copper wire proposed to be sold or delivered by the business to the salvage dealer.11.4( 4) If the Minister approves the application, the Minister shall issue a Class A licence to the applicant free of charge, and the licence has no expiry date.11.4( 5) If the Minister refuses the application, the Minister shall give the applicant a written notice giving reasons for the decision and informing the applicant of the right to have the decision reviewed. 11.4( 6) Within 30 days after receiving the written notice, the applicant may make a request to the Minister for a review of the decision.11.4( 7) Within 60 days after receiving the request for review, the Minister shall review the decision and either confirm or rescind it and give the applicant a written notice giving reasons for the decision.Class B licences11.5( 1) On application, the Minister may, free of charge, issue to the holder of any licence referred to in subsection 9(1) of New Brunswick Regulation 84-165 under the Electrical Installation and Inspection Act a Class B licence authorizing the holder to sell or deliver, as salvage, stranded copper wire consisting of less than eight strands to a salvage dealer.11.5( 2) An application for a Class B licence shall be made at the same time as the application for one of the licences referred to in subsection 9(1) of New Brunswick Regulation 84-165 under the Electrical Installation and Inspection Act.11.5( 3) A Class B licence held by a licence holder remains valid for as long as the licence held by that licence holder under subsection 9(1) of New Brunswick Regulation 84-165 under the Electrical Installation and Inspection Act remains valid.11.5( 4) If the Minister refuses the application, the Minister shall give the applicant a written notice giving reasons for the decision and informing the applicant of the right to have the decision reviewed. 11.5( 5) Within 30 days after receiving the written notice, the applicant may make a request to the Minister for a review of the decision.11.5( 6) Within 60 days after receiving the request for review, the Minister shall review the decision and either confirm or rescind it and give the applicant a written notice giving reasons for the decision.8
